What you can do with SocialEase

📖

Start from a template

Haircut, dentist, first day at school, flu jab, Christmas and more, ready to make your own.

🎙️

Your photos, your voice

Add your own photos, words, emoji and colours, and record narration for any page, so the story feels familiar and reassuring.

💌

Share with the people who help

Send a story to a grandparent, teacher or carer in a few taps. Opening and reading it is free for them.

🔒

Private by design

Your stories, photos and recordings stay on your device or in your own iCloud account, synced across your iPhone and iPad if you turn it on. We never see them, we don't track you, and we never share your data.
